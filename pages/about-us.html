Our Story

Founded in 2022, BookVerse started as a small idea between friends who shared a deep love for books and a frustration with the limited options for independent sellers.

Today, we've grown into a thriving community of over 500 vendors and 120,000 readers, spanning every genre.

We believe every book deserves to find its reader, and every reader deserves access to the widest possible selection.

Our Values
